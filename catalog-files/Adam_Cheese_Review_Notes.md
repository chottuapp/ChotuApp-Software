# Adam Cheese — Rule 804

58 products retained; no standalone promotional gift or missing/non-positive-price product was found. All 58 descriptions were rewritten into professional, product-specific English. Categories and subcategories were standardised, including dairy drinks for lassi, separate yoghurt and raita types, and bundle categories.

Full supplied cheese/butter ingredient lists are retained without guessing missing ingredient punctuation. Descriptions preserve pack sizes, full bundle contents, preparation/serving uses, source-supported nutrition and formulation details, and source refrigeration instructions at 4°C or 2°C–4°C. Storage conditions were not invented for products or bundles whose source description did not supply them. Irrelevant company history, duplicate claims and sales language were removed.

11 genuinely discounted product rows are sorted by percentage discount descending. Equal-discount and non-discounted products retain source relative order. Website access was blocked, so live best-selling order could not be verified; no popularity ranking was invented.

Source details not supplied: the Yogurt & Milk Deal does not state the quantity of Amson’s Dahi, and the Rawaiti Deal does not state individual component sizes. Their named contents are retained without guessed quantities. The Slice Combo description explicitly supplies a 1 kg cheddar block and a 1 kg burger-slice pack; those source quantities are preserved.

Validation: every retained product matches by ID for all fields other than category, subcategory and description. Prices, discounts, SKUs, URLs, images, availability and variant records are unchanged. All non-Products workbook ZIP parts are byte-identical to the uploaded workbook. Sheet names, headers and retained cell styles are unchanged. No formulas were present in the source.
